About

I am a researcher applying artificial intelligence to biomedical engineering, with a focus on cardiovascular health. My work covers advanced ECG analysis, early detection and classification of cardiac diseases, and robust signal and image processing.

I design multimodal deep learning models for classification and segmentation, combining different biomedical data sources to improve diagnostic accuracy. My goal is to close the gap between AI methods and clinical practice with solutions that are precise, efficient and interpretable.
